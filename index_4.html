The fire&faye story

Made by hand.
Meant to be felt.

fire&faye began as a dream — a belief that the jewellery you wear every day should carry more than just beauty. It should carry intention. Each piece is handcrafted using real natural gemstone beads and high-quality stainless steel, chosen for their energy, texture, and truth.

Nothing here is mass-produced. Everything is made with care, one piece at a time, in a space where creativity and the spiritual world meet. This is jewellery for the woman who feels things deeply.
